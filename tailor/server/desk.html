What the Desk does appears here.

How the Desk works. Postings come from PDFs, links, or the extension's Save to Desk button. Each one is read for its facts (deadline, years asked, sponsorship wording, pay, on-site days) and tailored with the same rules as the extension: your base resume, the bullet wordings you approved, one page, truth-checked. Nothing is sent to an employer; you upload the PDF yourself.
The server starts by itself at login and restarts if it stops (bash scripts/autostart.sh status to check).
Open the Desk: http://127.0.0.1:8765/desk, or Open Tailor Desk in the extension popup.
Save from a job page: open the posting, click the Tailor Assist icon, then Save posting to Desk.
Where files live: the queue in ~/project_tailor_data/desk/postings.json, resumes in ~/project_tailor_data/output/tailored/.
The Tailor Assist extension

To install: unzip it → open chrome://extensions → turn on Developer mode → Load unpacked → pick the TailorAssist folder → in the extension's Options, paste your access token (~/project_tailor_data/server_token.txt).
Updating: replace the folder's files with the new ones, then click the reload arrow ↻ on chrome://extensions. It works where this server runs (it talks to 127.0.0.1:8765 only).
Links: reading a link is the only time the Desk goes outside your computer: it opens that one page, as your browser would. Links to this computer or your home network are refused. Sites that need a login (Workday, some campus portals) cannot be read this way: use the extension's Save to Desk while you are signed in.
